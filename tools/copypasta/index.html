EVE copy paste tools

Local-first helpers for text copied to and from the EVE Online client. Select a tool from the nav bar to open it below.

Tools run entirely in your browser and keep their state when you switch panes. No ESI access or EVE account is required. All state is lost when the page reloads.

Multi-buy

Paste inventory or a material list, track what you have on hand, multiply material sets, and export a multi-buy list of only the items you still need.

Open standalone

Industry Materials

Convert material lists copied from an EVE Industry activity into Markdown, including activity details and a combined Multibuy table.
